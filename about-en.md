## What I do

I build and operate my own platform for secure AI usage in enterprises — from LLM gateways with PII detection through compliance shields to local inference and agent infrastructure. The goal: make AI usable where data must not enter public clouds — banks, insurers, law firms, B2B SaaS, public sector.

Most AI solutions remain demos. I build systems that run in regulated environments and satisfy audit obligations. That's where the combination of AI engineering, cloud governance and compliance is rarest — and delivers the most leverage.

In parallel, I modernize legacy business software and integrate B2B systems.
